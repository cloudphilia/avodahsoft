Every document, in one place.

Write the note. Scan the page, fill in the form, sign the PDF, and ask your own notes what you said last month. Set the reminder beside it, and keep your habits there too. PaperSuite holds every document in one place, on your iPhone and iPad.

Notify me at launch What it does

Notes, documents and habits

Write it. Scan it. Sign it.

Notes, PDFs, reminders and habits in one app, so the paperwork of a week stops living in five.

Notes that keep up

Headings, checklists, colour and photos as you type. Folders, pins and today’s reminders sit at the top, so the note you need is the first one you see. Export any note as text or PDF.

Fill in and sign PDFs

Type into the form, draw your signature once and place it on the page in black or blue ink. It stays a real PDF, ready to send back. Part of PaperSuite Pro.

Every PDF tool in one place

Scan pages with Apple’s own document scanner, turn photos into PDFs, merge files, and convert Word, Excel and more.

Ask your notes anything

Ask a question with PaperSuite Pro and get an answer drawn only from your own writing. When your notes do not know, it says so instead of making something up.

Reminders that ring

Put a time on anything worth remembering. The reminder rings when it should, and stays beside the note it belongs to.

Habits and streaks

Daily check-ins, counted targets and 7, 30, 60 or 90-day challenges, with a month at a glance that shows what you actually did.

Why PaperSuite

One place for everything on paper.

Most of what we keep is paper in some form: the notes from the meeting, the lease to sign, the form to send back, and the reminder to do it by Friday. They usually live in five different apps. PaperSuite keeps them together, on your phone, and lets you ask your own notes what you wrote, with answers drawn only from your writing.

Privacy

Private by design. Yours, on your phone.

  • No account needed. Sign in with Apple only if you want your notes and habits backed up and on your other devices.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking software of any kind.
  • Scans, photos, PDFs and signatures stay on the phone, and are shared only when you choose to share them.
  • Ask AI asks your permission before anything is sent, and sends only what that question needs.
