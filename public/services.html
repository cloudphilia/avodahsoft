Process

Four stages. No surprises.

Client work remains confidential unless a client chooses otherwise. The process is the same for every engagement.

Scope

Screens, states and exclusions are documented, and a fixed price is agreed against them before work begins.

Build

A working build on your own device every week, so issues surface early and decisions stay yours.

Release

App Store listing, privacy disclosures and App Review are part of the engagement, not an extra.

Handover
