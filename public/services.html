How a project runs

Four steps. No surprises.

Client work is not listed publicly unless the client wants it to be. What is worth saying is how it goes.

Scope, written down

Screens, states and what is explicitly out. A fixed price against that document, so the number does not move unless the scope does.

A build you can hold

Every week, installed on your own device rather than a screenshot in a deck. You see it going wrong early enough to say so.

Submission included

App Store listing, privacy labels, review responses. The job is not done when the code is done.

You own all of it

Repository, accounts, signing, the lot, documented well enough that another developer could pick it up without ringing us.

Acquisitions

Buy an app outright.

When one of our apps would grow faster with a new owner, it is for sale, in whole or in part. You get a clean codebase, a documented handover, whatever revenue history exists, and a transition long enough that nothing breaks on your watch.

Enquiries get a real reply rather than a calendar link.
