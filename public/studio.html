Principles

Quality is in the details.

The empty state, the error message, the second tap that should have been one. Few people notice them, and they decide whether software feels finished. We hold every product to that standard, ours or yours.

Direct access

You deal directly with engineering, without account managers or intermediaries.

Fixed scope and price

Scope is agreed in writing before work begins, and the price changes only with your approval.

Weekly builds

Working software on your own device every week, from the first week.

Full ownership

Code, accounts and signing keys are yours, documented for any developer to continue.
