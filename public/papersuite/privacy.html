Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026 · PaperSuite for iPhone · Operated by Avodahsoft, Australia

In one paragraph. PaperSuite works without an account, and your notes, PDFs, scans, signatures, photos, reminders and habits are kept on your iPhone. If you choose to sign in with Apple, the text of your notes and your habits are synced to your account so they are backed up and on your other devices; photos and PDFs stay on the phone. If you use Ask AI or the writing tools, the text needed for that request is sent to our server and to Anthropic to produce the answer. We do not keep it, and Anthropic keeps it only briefly for abuse monitoring. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and you can delete your account and everything synced with it from inside the app.

Who we are

PaperSuite is made and operated by Avodahsoft, a software company based in Australia. You can reach us at hello@avodahsoft.com. We are the data controller for the personal information described here.

What PaperSuite keeps on your device

Your notes and folders, reminders, habits and their history, diary entries, PDFs you scan, import or save, signatures you draw, photos you attach to notes and your settings are stored in the app’s own storage on your iPhone. iOS encrypts that storage whenever the phone is locked. Unless you sign in, none of it leaves the device, except in iPhone backups (iCloud or a computer) that you control.

Locked notes open with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode through iOS. PaperSuite never receives biometric data. A lock hides a note inside PaperSuite; it is not separate encryption, and a locked note is kept out of exports, search previews, notifications and Ask AI.

If you sign in

An account is optional and uses Sign in with Apple. When you sign in we store:

  • Account details: the email address Apple shares with us (which can be Apple’s private relay address if you choose to hide yours), your name if you share it or have set one in PaperSuite, an emoji avatar if you choose one, and a random account identifier. A profile photo you choose stays on your device and is not uploaded.
  • What syncs: the text of your notes (title, body, folder, colour, icon, pin and lock status, dates), your habits, completions and diary entries, and your settings, so they are backed up and available on your other devices.
  • What does not sync: photos, scans, PDFs and signatures stay on the device. Reminder alerts are scheduled on each device.

When you first sign in, what you wrote on the phone before signing in joins your account. Accounts and synced data are held by our database provider, Supabase, on servers in South Korea, protected by per-account access rules so that one account can never read another’s data.

Ask AI and the writing tools

These features are optional and part of PaperSuite Pro. When you use one, the text needed for that request is sent over HTTPS to our server and from there to Anthropic, whose Claude model writes the answer. For a question, that is the question, up to 12 of your notes (the ones most relevant to it, or your most recent), the names and daily targets of your habits, and the last few questions and answers of the conversation. For the writing tools, it is the note you are working in, any words you selected in it, and your instruction. Ask never includes locked notes; the writing tools send a locked note only when you have opened it and ask them to. The pictures in your notes are never included. The first time you use one of these features, PaperSuite asks your permission before anything is sent, and you can withdraw it at any time in Profile (“Allow Ask AI”). To count requests against your monthly allowance, our server records how many requests an identifier made each day; that identifier is a random one the app creates for you, or your account if you have signed in. So that one subscription cannot be used to get around the allowance, the server also keeps a daily count for each subscription, identified by the store, the product and the date it was first bought; that count is not linked to an account. We do not store the content of your requests or the answers. Anthropic processes them under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on them, and keeps them only briefly for abuse monitoring.

AI answers can be wrong. PaperSuite never applies one to your notes by itself: it is shown to you first, and nothing changes until you choose.

PaperSuite Pro and purchases

PaperSuite Pro is an auto-renewing subscription sold through Apple. Apple processes the payment and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active: it receives your purchase records from Apple and the app’s random identifier, and tells our server whether Pro is active.

App updates

When it starts, PaperSuite checks Expo’s update service for fixes and downloads them. The check includes the app’s version and platform and, like any web request, your IP address. It contains no account details or note content. Updates are signed, and the app refuses one that is not.

Device permissions

  • Camera: to scan documents, to read the text on a page into a note (Scan text), and to take photos, only when you choose to. Scan text recognises the words on your iPhone; the page is not sent anywhere and is deleted once read.
  • Photos: to add pictures to a note or turn them into a PDF, only the ones you pick.
  • Notifications: for the reminders you set. They are scheduled on your device; a locked note’s reminder shows only “Note”.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: to open notes you have locked.

PaperSuite does not use the microphone or your location.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal information with data brokers.
  • We do not use your notes to train AI models, and neither does Anthropic.

Retention and deletion

Notes go to Recently Deleted and are removed after 30 days, or straight away if you empty it. You can delete your account from the Profile screen; this permanently removes your account and everything synced with it from our servers at once. If you signed in with Apple, it also revokes PaperSuite’s access to your Apple ID, and it asks RevenueCat to delete its record of your identifier. The daily per-subscription AI counts described above, which are not linked to an account, are kept so that deleting an account cannot reset the allowance. Deleting your account does not cancel a PaperSuite Pro subscription: cancel it in your iPhone’s Settings, under your name and Subscriptions. What is on the phone is removed when you delete the app; export your notes first if you want a copy. To ask for deletion or a copy of your data by email, write to hello@avodahsoft.com.

Security

All traffic between the app and our services is encrypted with HTTPS. Your sign-in session is kept in the iPhone’s keychain, on that device only. Database access is restricted with row-level security so that every request is limited to the signed-in account, and the keys to the AI service never leave our server. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected users if we become aware of a breach involving their personal information.

Children

PaperSuite is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Australian users are covered by the Privacy Act 1988; users in the EU and UK by the GDPR. Most of these rights can be exercised directly in the app; for anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell you inside the app before the change takes effect.
