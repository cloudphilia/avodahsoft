What GigPal is

GigPal is a practice tool for musicians. It analyses songs you add for key, tempo, chords and structure, lets you change speed and pitch, provides a metronome, tuner, piano, recorder, lyrics tools and setlists, and, with a Pro subscription, separates songs into stems in the cloud.
