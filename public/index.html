What we do

Build. Ship. Hand over.

We build your app

You have the idea and know the market. We handle design, development, App Store submission and the first release. Fixed scope, fixed price, and something working in your hands every week.

We run our own

Products released under the studio’s name and supported by the person who wrote them. No ticket queue. You email the studio and the studio answers.

We hand over properly

When an app finds the right owner: a clean codebase, documented handover, whatever revenue history exists, and a transition long enough that nothing breaks on your watch.

Approach

Finished, not merely working.

Most software ships at the point it stops erroring. We are interested in the part after that: the copy that says what it means, the state nobody planned for, the second tap that should have been one. You deal with the person doing the work, and the work is done in the open.
