How we work

Most software ships when it stops erroring. Ours ships when it is finished.

The copy that says what it means, the state nobody planned for, the second tap that should have been one. That is the part we are interested in, and it is done in the open.

Straight to engineering

No account managers and no game of telephone. Your questions go directly to the people writing the code, and so do your answers.

Scope in writing, price fixed to it

Screens, states and what is deliberately left out. The price moves only when the document does, and then you decide.

Working software every week

On your own device, not in a slide deck, so you can say something is wrong while it is still cheap to change.

Everything handed over

Repository, accounts, signing keys and notes explaining every decision. You are never held hostage by whoever wrote it.
