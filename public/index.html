How we work

A written scope. A working build every week. Everything handed over at the end.

Every engagement follows the same process, whether the product is ours or yours.

Direct access

You deal directly with engineering, without account managers or intermediaries, so decisions are quick and nothing is lost in translation.

Fixed scope and price

Screens, states and exclusions are agreed in writing before work begins. The price changes only if the scope does, and only with your approval.

Weekly builds

A working build on your own device every week, so feedback arrives while changes are still inexpensive.

Full ownership

You own the code, accounts and signing keys, with documentation thorough enough for any developer to continue the work.
