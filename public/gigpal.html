Features

One app. The whole practice room.

Built for gigging musicians, worship teams, choirs, students and anyone who learns songs by ear.

Stem mixer

Separate a song into vocals, piano and keys, bass, drums and everything else. Solo the part you are learning or mute the part you play.

Chords that follow the song

The chord progression is detected on your device and highlighted bar by bar as the song plays. Read it as letters, as numbers (1, 4, 5, 7♭) or as solfa.

Key and tempo

Every song is analysed for key, tempo and beats the moment you add it. Re-analyse in a tap if you disagree, and see it laid out on a piano.

Speed and pitch

Slow a passage to half speed without changing the pitch, or move the whole song into your singing key. Both stay locked to the chords and lyrics.

Sections and loops

GigPal finds the intro, verses and choruses. Tap a section to jump to it or loop it until it is under your fingers.

Lyrics finder

Pull in synced lyrics for the song you are working on and follow them as they scroll. Save any lyrics you find, or paste ones you read elsewhere, and edit them later.

Metronome

A rock-solid metronome with tap tempo, count-in, accents and subdivisions, designed to be heard over a full band.

Tuner

A chromatic tuner with a clear needle and cents readout, accurate to the cent on guitar and bass, low strings included. Works for voice, brass and strings too.

Piano and chord keys

A sampled grand piano on iPhone and iPad. One key plays a whole chord in your key, with pads 1–8, passing chords and a soft synth pad underneath. Turn the phone sideways for a full keyboard, and solo over chords in two rows.

Recorder

Record yourself over the mix or on its own. Every take is analysed for key and tempo too, so you can see how the rehearsal went.

Setlists

Group songs into setlists for a gig or a service, give each set a date and each song a note, reorder by drag, and Play set runs through them in order.

Play Along

Paste a YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music or SoundCloud link and play along with the tuner, metronome and piano on top. Every link you play is kept, ready to name.

Why GigPal

Every practice tool. One app.

Musicians usually rehearse with a chord app, a metronome, a tuner and the song open in a browser tab. GigPal brings them together on one screen, keeps them in sync, and stays out of the way while you play.

How it works

From file to rehearsal in a minute.

Add a song

From your files, your camera roll, a link, or a fresh recording. Demo songs are included so you can try everything first.

Analysed on your phone

Key, tempo, beats, chords and sections are detected on the device itself. Nothing leaves your phone for this step.

Separate the stems

The song is separated in the cloud using the same open-source model family the big apps rely on, then deleted from our servers.

Practise

Mix the parts, follow the chords, slow it down, loop it, record yourself. Then add it to the setlist for Sunday.

Pricing

GigPal is free. Stems included.

Everything that runs on your phone is free. Cloud stem separation costs real computing time, so version 1.0 includes it with a fair-use limit of three songs a day per account. A GigPal Pro plan with a higher allowance is planned for a later update and will be billed through the App Store.

Privacy

Private by design. Your music stays yours.

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking. Analysis runs on your device. Songs you separate are uploaded over an encrypted connection, processed, returned to you and deleted from our servers. You can delete your account and everything with it from inside the app.
