Everything a rehearsal needs

One app, the whole practice room.

Built for gigging musicians, worship teams, choirs, students and anyone who learns songs by ear.

Stem mixer

Separate a song into vocals, piano and keys, bass, drums and everything else. Solo the part you are learning, mute the part you play, or split the vocals into lead and backing.

Chords that follow the song

The chord progression is detected on your device and highlighted bar by bar as the song plays. Read it as letters, as numbers (1, 4, 5, 7♭) or as solfa.

Key and tempo

Every song is analysed for key, tempo and beats the moment you add it. Re-analyse in a tap if you disagree, and see it laid out on a piano.

Speed and pitch

Slow a passage to half speed without changing the pitch, or move the whole song into your singing key. Both stay locked to the chords and lyrics.

Sections and loops

GigPal finds the intro, verses and choruses. Tap a section to jump to it or loop it until it is under your fingers.

Lyrics finder

Pull in synced lyrics for the song you are working on, follow them as they scroll, and translate chord symbols in your own charts to numbers or solfa.

Metronome

A rock-solid metronome with tap tempo, count-in, accents and subdivisions, designed to be heard over a full band.

Tuner

A chromatic tuner with a clear needle and cents readout. Works for voice, guitar, bass, brass and strings.

Piano and solfa

A sampled grand piano to check a note or find a starting pitch, plus a solfa trainer for singers who read do-re-mi.

Recorder

Record yourself over the mix or on its own. Every take is analysed for key and tempo too, so you can see how the rehearsal went.

Setlists

Group songs into setlists for a gig or a service, reorder them by drag, and move through the set without leaving the player.

Reminders and links

Set practice reminders, and add songs from your files, camera roll, a direct link, or play along with YouTube, Spotify and Apple Music.

Why we built it

Made by a musician who was tired of switching apps.

Chords in one app, a metronome in another, a tuner in a third, and the actual song in a browser tab. GigPal puts them on one screen, keeps them in sync, and stays out of your way when you are playing.

How it works

From file to rehearsal in a minute.

Add a song

From your files, your camera roll, a link, or a fresh recording. Demo songs are included so you can try everything first.

Analysed on your phone

Key, tempo, beats, chords and sections are detected on the device itself. Nothing leaves your phone for this step.

Separate the stems

With GigPal Pro, the song is separated in the cloud using the same open-source model family the big apps rely on, then deleted from our servers.

Practise

Mix the parts, follow the chords, slow it down, loop it, record yourself. Then add it to the setlist for Sunday.

Free and Pro

Most of GigPal is free. Stems are Pro.

Everything that runs on your phone stays free forever. Cloud stem separation costs real computing time, so it lives in a GigPal Pro subscription billed through the App Store. Pricing will be announced at launch and you can cancel any time in your Apple settings.

Your music stays yours.

No advertising, no analytics SDKs, no tracking. Analysis runs on your device. Songs you separate are uploaded over an encrypted connection, processed, returned to you and deleted from our servers. You can delete your account and everything with it from inside the app.
